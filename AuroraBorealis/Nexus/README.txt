[size=6][b]Aurora Borealis[/b][/size]

Shimmering curtains of northern lights dance across the Valheim night sky. Fades in at dusk, out at dawn, and is hidden by rain and snow.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Layered, rippling curtains of light with moving rays and a sharp glowing lower edge
[*]Five colour schemes: Classic green, Violet, Ice, Crimson and Rainbow
[*]Fades in at dusk and out at dawn (or show it all day)
[*]Hidden while it rains or snows, and when you are indoors
[*]Choose where it hangs in the sky, how wide it stretches, how tall and how fast it moves
[*]Client side only: no need for the server or other players to have it
[/list]


[size=5][b]Configuration[/b][/size]

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[b]Automatic Install[/b]

Plugin is created to be installed with r2modman or Gale.
For Nexus downloads you will need to install manually.

[b]Manual Install[/b]

[list]
[*]Un-zip and drag the [b]AuroraBorealis[/b] folder in your [b]BepInEx/plugins/[/b] folder.
[/list]
